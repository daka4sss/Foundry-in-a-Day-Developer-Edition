# Code of Conduct

**English** | [日本語](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

This workshop is conducted in accordance with the **Microsoft Events Code of Conduct**.

---

## Microsoft's Commitment

Microsoft's mission is to empower every person and every organization on the planet to achieve more.
This includes seeking to create a respectful, friendly, enjoyable, and inclusive experience for all participants at every Microsoft event, including conferences and events held on digital platforms.

---

## Expected Behavior

- Support Microsoft's mission to empower every person and every organization on the planet to achieve more
- Apply these expectations to all event-related activities, both on digital platforms and in person
- Follow the principles of this Code of Conduct
- Do not tolerate disruptive or disrespectful behavior, messages, images, videos, or interactions in any part of the program, including business activities and social interactions, regardless of location

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or discrimination based on age, ancestry, color, gender identity or expression, national origin, physical or mental disability, religion, sexual orientation, or any other characteristic protected by applicable local laws, regulations, or ordinances
- Violent behavior or threats, suspicious behavior, or offensive conduct
- Harassment of any kind toward organizers, staff, speakers, or participants

---

## Reporting and Contact Information

Report concerns, harassment, suspicious behavior, or offensive conduct to venue staff, the event host or owner, or the nearest event staff member.
Microsoft employees should contact a Microsoft manager or the Business Conduct Hotline whenever they believe a participant has violated this policy.
